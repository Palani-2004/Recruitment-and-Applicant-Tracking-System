# Recruitment and Applicant Tracking System

## Project Overview
The Recruitment and Applicant Tracking System is a recruitment management application developed using Zoho Creator. It helps organize applicant information, manage recruitment activities, schedule interviews, and track candidate progress throughout the selection process.

## Key Features

- **Applicant Registration:** Collect and maintain applicant personal, educational, project, and internship details.
- **Company Management:** Maintain company information and contact details.
- **Interview Management:** Configure interview rounds and manage interview roles.
- **Interview Scheduling:** Schedule interviews and track their status.
- **Application Tracking:** Maintain interview scores, round status, and interviewer comments.
- **Application Dashboard:** Centralized dashboard for monitoring recruitment activities.

## Modules
- Registration Form
- Company Master
- Interview Rounds Master
- Interview Roles
- Interview Schedule
- Application Status and Information Tracker
- Application Dashboard
